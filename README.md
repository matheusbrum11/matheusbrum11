6+ years building and scaling native Android apps in **Kotlin** and **Java**, focused on
**fintech** and large-scale enterprise products used by millions of people.

I specialize in the unglamorous work that keeps big apps alive: leading full architectural
migrations (Java→Kotlin, MVC/MVP→MVVM/MVI), breaking monoliths into multi-module and
multi-repository codebases, building offline-first sync engines that actually reconcile, and
taking test coverage from **zero to production-grade quality gates**.

Currently a technical lead for Android at **Avanade**, on the digital banking app of a major
Brazilian bank.

### What I bring

- **Architecture at scale** — Clean Architecture, MVVM/MVI, multi-module & multi-repo, Dynamic
  Feature Modules, shared modules published as internal libraries via JFrog/Artifactory.
- **Offline-first** — sync engines with Coroutines/Flow + WorkManager and exponential backoff;
  cut recurring sync and data-inconsistency issues by ~70% on a mobile ERP.
- **Quality as infrastructure** — TDD/GWT, JUnit, Mockk, MockWebServer; CI/CD gates at ~90%
  coverage with SonarQube, static analysis and architecture enforcement.
- **Design systems & white-label** — built an Android Design System from scratch, evolved it
  from XML to Compose, themed per client through build flavors.
- **AI-assisted delivery** — built an AI agent for test generation that helped reach real 90%
  coverage; agent-assisted workflows sped up delivery by roughly 50%.
